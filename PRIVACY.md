# Privacy Policy for Markpad Native

**Effective Date: May 20, 2026**

## Overview
Markpad Native ("the Application") is a local, offline text utility developed by Vodiy ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Application.

## Data Collection and Use
Markpad Native is designed to operate entirely offline on your local device. 

**We do not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal information, usage metrics, telemetry, user credentials, or file contents.** 

All file operations, parsing heuristics, and syntax rendering happen entirely locally on your Windows PC. The Application does not establish any external network connections back to our servers or any third-party analytics services.
