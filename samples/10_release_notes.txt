MARKPAD NATIVE v2.0.0 — OFFICIAL RELEASE NOTES
Published by Vodiy | September 2026
======================================================================

We are thrilled to announce Markpad Native v2.0.0!

This milestone release brings complete multi-window cross-drag-and-drop,
a redesigned vector brand icon, instant Office and PDF reading, and
deep internationalization.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.0.0:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Professional App Icon (Mark "Down"):
   - Modern geometric "M" with the right leg forming a crisp downward pointer.
   - Beautiful Electric Violet to Neon Cyan gradient.
   - Clean, pixel-perfect rendering across all Windows & macOS icon sizes.

2. True Cross-Window Tab Dragging:
   - Tear off any tab to spawn a new native window at your cursor position.
   - Seamlessly drag tabs between running windows with live insertion glow.
   - Single-instance IPC architecture prevents duplicate window chaos.

3. Complete Office & PDF Viewer Suite:
   - Open Word (.docx) documents offline with formatting preserved.
   - Inspect Excel (.xlsx, .xls) spreadsheets in an interactive sheet viewer.
   - View, search, and print PDF documents with native PDF.js integration.

4. 7 Languages Supported:
   - English, Russian, Uzbek, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, and Spanish.

5. Direct Crypto & Community Donations:
   - 1-Click copy for TON and Solana wallet addresses.
   - Buy Me a Coffee and Google Form feedback survey integrated.
