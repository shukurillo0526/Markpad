Designed for Uncompromising Performance

Every feature in Markpad Native is purpose-built to solve real daily workflow headaches.

⚡

Sub-Millisecond Native Architecture

Built with native Rust and Tauri v2. Opens in the blink of an eye, uses under 35 MB of RAM, and smoothly opens multi-megabyte files without freezing your desktop.

📊

All-in-One Office & Document Suite

Native multi-sheet Excel spreadsheet editor, rich Word (.docx) document editor, and 100% offline PDF viewer built right in—no Microsoft Office or Acrobat required.

🌲

Interactive Code, JSON & Data Tools

Explore JSON and YAML as collapsible visual trees, browse massive CSV/TSV tables with instant pagination, inspect environment variables with secret masking, and filter log files on the fly.

🔒

True Zero-Cloud Local Privacy

Your proprietary code, business spreadsheets, and private notes never leave your machine. Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero network calls, and zero account sign-ups.

🖨️

Studio-Grade PDF & HTML Export

Export markdown documents to crisp, publication-ready PDF files with custom margins via the native print engine, or copy standalone formatted HTML with embedded styles in one click.

🪟

Modern Tabbed Productivity

Detachable multi-window architecture, drag-and-drop tab reordering, pin tabs, CodeMirror syntax highlighting, in-document Find/Replace in 7 languages, and distraction-free dark & light modes.
